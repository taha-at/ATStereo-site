Precision Bilateral Stereotactic Planning for 3D Slicer

ATStereo is a research non-commercial licensed medical software module designed for dual stereotactic frame registration, multi-path trajectory simulation, and real-time kinematic calculations in 3D Slicer.

Clinical Overview

What is ATStereo?

Designed for stereotactic neurosurgery, ATStereo provides end-to-end trajectory planning capabilities within 3D Slicer across three core clinical phases.

Phase 1: Registration

4-Point Landmark Alignment

Aligns the virtual stereotactic coordinate frame directly to patient CT imaging via four anatomical fiducial landmarks with immediate root-mean-square error (RMSE) verification.

  • Standardized Isocenter marking order
  • Rigid body registration with RMSE feedback
Phase 2: Trajectory Planning

Bilateral Trajectory Kinematics

Simulates simultaneous dual-hemisphere target and entry trajectories, projecting virtual stereotactic cannulas in 3D Slicer and computing physical arc and ring angles.

  • Target & entry coordinate computation
  • Simultaneous bilateral multi-path planning
Phase 3: Kinematics & Safety

Frame Angles & Collision Guard

Provides real-time mathematical validation of arc and ring orientations, with active proximity monitoring that triggers a safety warning if inter-needle distance drops below 5.0 mm.

  • Calculated Arc & Ring angle result table
  • Automated collision guard (< 5.0 mm warning)

Video Tutorial #1

Module Download & 3D Slicer Setup

Step-by-step walkthrough on downloading 3D Slicer, extracting the ATStereo module, and adding the module path in settings.

1

Install 3D Slicer

Download and install 3D Slicer (v5.12+) from the official website for your operating system.

Open download.slicer.org →
2

Download & Extract the Module Folder

Download the ATStereo module zip from the latest GitHub release and extract the folder into a permanent directory on your computer.

Get Latest Release (.zip) →
3

Register Module Path in Slicer

Launch 3D Slicer and navigate through the top menu:

Edit → Application Settings → Modules

In Additional module paths, click Add and select your extracted ATStereo directory.

4

Restart & Search for ATStereo

Restart 3D Slicer. In the module search bar at the top, type ATStereo and click to open the interface.

Video Tutorial #2

Fiducial Registration & Trajectory Planning

Complete surgical workflow: loading patient CT imaging, marking the 4 fiducial points, rigid body registration, and bilateral trajectory planning.

Crucial: Exact 4-Point Marking Order

When clicking "Step 1: Pick 4 points" in the ATStereo Workflow tab, mark the anatomical landmarks in this strict sequence:

1
Left Isocenter
Marker Point 1
2
Right Isocenter
Marker Point 2
3
Left Point
(0, 0, 120 mm)
4
Right Point
(0, 0, 120 mm)
1

Load Volume & Show 3D

In the Data Import section, load your NIfTI (.nii / .nii.gz) or DICOM dataset, or click Test. Click Show 3D to render.

2

Pick the 4 Fiducial Points

Click Step 1: Pick 4 points. Place the 4 landmarks on the slice or 3D view following the Left/Right isocenter order shown on the left.

3

Align to Isocenters & Check RMSE

Click Step 2: Align to Isocenters. The algorithm aligns the virtual frame coordinates to the patient anatomy. Verify the popup RMSE registration error in millimeters (e.g. 0.4 mm).

4

Set Target & Entry Coordinates

Sequentially click:

  • Step 3 & 4: Left Target & Left Entry (or Right Side)
  • Step 5 & 6: Set opposing side target & entry points

The module computes the stereotactic trajectory line through the skull and brain.

5

3D Frame Kinematics & Arc Angles

Inspect the computed Result Table: Local X, Y, Z coordinates, Arc Angle, and Ring Angle (e.g. Arc: 8.29°, Ring: 27.22°). Click Visualize Frame to verify physical arc alignment in the 3D viewport.

Safety Note: ATStereo also automatically monitors inter-needle distance (< 5.0 mm collision guard) whenever dual bilateral trajectories are loaded.
